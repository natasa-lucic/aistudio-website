## Overview

The permitting landscape is one of the most document-intensive processes in infrastructure delivery. Every project requires navigating a maze of federal, state, and local regulations — each with their own submission requirements, review timelines, and approval chains.

Our AI Studio team partnered with Arcadis environmental consultants to understand where the greatest friction existed. We discovered that over 40% of delays were caused not by regulatory review itself, but by incorrect submissions, missing cross-references, and routing errors.

## Technical Approach

The solution combines several AI capabilities into a unified workflow platform:

**Document Intelligence** — Custom NLP models trained on thousands of historical permit documents to automatically classify document types, extract key entities (project locations, species mentions, regulatory references), and identify missing information.

**Smart Routing** — A rules engine enhanced with machine learning that determines which agencies need to review each document, in what order, and with what dependencies.

**Predictive Flagging** — Historical approval data powers a model that identifies submissions likely to be rejected or delayed, allowing teams to address issues proactively.

## Results

The platform has fundamentally changed how permitting teams work. Instead of spending days organizing and routing documents, consultants now focus on the high-value work of resolving complex regulatory questions and engaging with stakeholders.
