## Overview

Infrastructure asset management faces a fundamental tension: replace assets too early and you waste capital; replace them too late and you risk catastrophic failure. For water treatment plants, bridges, and transportation systems, the consequences of getting this wrong range from costly emergency repairs to public safety risks.

The AI Studio partnered with Arcadis asset management specialists to develop a data-driven approach that moves beyond calendar-based maintenance to true condition-based and predictive maintenance.

## Technical Approach

**Sensor Integration** — The platform ingests data from diverse IoT sensors: vibration monitors, flow meters, temperature probes, water quality analyzers, and structural health monitors. Edge computing handles initial processing and anomaly detection at the source.

**Predictive Models** — Ensemble machine learning models trained on historical failure data, maintenance records, and environmental conditions estimate remaining useful life for each monitored asset. Models account for asset-specific characteristics, operating conditions, and degradation patterns.

**Digital Twin Integration** — Physical assets are represented as digital twins that combine real-time sensor data with engineering models. This enables "what-if" analysis: what happens if we defer maintenance by 3 months? What's the impact of changing operating parameters?

**Prioritization Engine** — Given limited maintenance budgets and crew availability, the system generates optimized maintenance schedules that balance risk, cost, and operational constraints.

## Results

The platform has transformed how asset managers make decisions. Instead of relying on manufacturer recommendations and engineering judgment alone, they now have data-driven confidence in their maintenance priorities. The 12 prevented critical failures in year one alone justified the entire investment.
