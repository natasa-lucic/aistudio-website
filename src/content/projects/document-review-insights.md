## Overview

Infrastructure projects are document-intensive by nature. A single large project can generate tens of thousands of pages across environmental studies, engineering reports, stakeholder correspondence, and regulatory filings. The challenge isn't just volume — it's that critical information is often scattered across documents, buried in technical appendices, or expressed differently by different authors.

Our AI Studio identified this as one of the highest-impact opportunities: turning unstructured document collections into structured, queryable knowledge bases that preserve context and provenance.

## Technical Approach

**Multi-format Ingestion** — The platform handles PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, CAD annotations, and scanned images through a unified processing pipeline. OCR and layout analysis ensure nothing is missed, even in poorly formatted legacy documents.

**Intelligent Extraction** — Fine-tuned language models extract structured data from unstructured text: measurements, dates, regulatory references, risk factors, species mentions, and spatial references. Each extraction is linked back to its source with page-level citations.

**Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — When team members ask questions, the system retrieves relevant passages from across the document collection, synthesizes an answer, and provides citations. This ensures accuracy while making the knowledge accessible through natural language.

**Knowledge Graph** — Extracted entities and relationships are mapped into a graph structure, enabling discovery of cross-document connections that would be invisible to manual reviewers.

## Results

The platform has become an essential tool for project delivery teams. New team members can get up to speed on complex projects in hours rather than weeks. Critical risks identified in one document are automatically connected to related findings elsewhere. The institutional knowledge that previously walked out the door with departing employees is now preserved and accessible.
